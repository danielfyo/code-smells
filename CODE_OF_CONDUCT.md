# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link applies; this page summarises it.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for people learning.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainer,
[@danielfyo](https://github.com/danielfyo), using the contact details on their GitHub profile.
All reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
